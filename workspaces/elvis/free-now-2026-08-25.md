# Free Now, scoping (from `feature-backlog-2026-08-25.md` item 3)

> Elvis workspace working file, started 2026-08-25. Full scoping done same day, third item picked
> from the 2026-08-25 batch intake, flagged from the start as the highest safety consideration in the
> whole batch.
>
> No em-dashes. Governance values ALLOW / BLOCK / ESCALATE.

## Problem

Users mark themselves as free right now, with a location and a duration, and join location-tied
chatrooms with others who are also free, to plan something spontaneous. One always-on public room per
city, created by WePop; users can also create their own, pinned to a more specific location.

## Why this needed more care than anything else in the batch

Real-time location plus open or semi-open chat has a documented failure pattern across other
products, not a hypothetical one. Snapchat's Snap Map drew real stalking concerns and ships heavy
location obfuscation by default. Yik Yak, an anonymous location-based social app, effectively
collapsed under harassment it could not moderate. Proximity features on dating apps have documented
physical-safety incidents and now mostly round or grid-snap displayed location specifically to
prevent one person triangulating another's exact position. WePop's own stated brand position is
privacy-by-design and anti-stalking (`shared/PROJECT_STRATEGY.md`); this feature is the one place in
the current roadmap where that positioning is actually tested, not just stated.

## A new, third top-level concept, not an Idea or an Event

Free Now is not a variant of Idea (a proposed activity someone can spin into an event) or Event (a
scheduled, committed activity). It is real-time personal presence plus a location-tied chat, no
commitment, no schedule, expires on its own. Worth stating plainly so it isn't quietly folded into
either existing model during build.

## Location precision, RESOLVED 2026-08-25: rounded/approximate, not an exact pin

A user's set location, for both their own broadcast and for placing them into a room, snaps to a
general area rather than an exact coordinate. Matches DEC-003's own precedent that profile-level
location stays coarse (general city only), precision is reserved for events people deliberately
commit to attending. Also matches the standard safety mitigation comparable products use against
exact-location triangulation.

## Room visibility, RESOLVED 2026-08-25: aggregate first, identities on reciprocal join

Two distinct levels, refined 2026-08-25 from the first pass:

- **Browsing the list of Free Now rooms** (which rooms exist, roughly how many people are free in
  each) is open to any user, no need to mark yourself free first.
- **Seeing individual identities inside a specific room, or joining its chat**, requires the viewer to
  also mark themselves free in that room. Reciprocal by design, the same principle already locked for
  mutual-follow photo visibility in item 8: nobody gets to see who's around without being visible
  themselves. Prevents one-sided browsing of a room's actual participants.

## Avatar status badge, RESOLVED 2026-08-25: binary indicator, followers/mutuals only

Marking yourself Free Now shows a status badge on your profile avatar wherever it appears, similar to
Instagram's active-status indicator. The badge shows only that the person is currently free, no
location or room name attached to it, so finding out where still requires going through the room
mechanism above. Visible only to followers and mutuals, not the general public, keeping this new
surface consistent with the reciprocity-first posture the rest of the feature is built on.

## Room creation, RESOLVED 2026-08-25: requires account standing beyond phone verification

Creating a pinned Free Now room (as opposed to marking yourself free inside an existing room, which
only requires standard phone verification per DEC-004) requires additional account standing. Exact
threshold not yet set, a reasonable starting point is something like a minimum account age plus at
least one verified real-world action (for example, having actually checked into an event), but this
needs Elvis's final number before build, flagged below rather than decided here. Creating a room is
functionally closer to standing up an open public chatroom tied to a real physical location than it is
to a normal user action, and carries real moderation exposure a throwaway account should not have easy
access to.

## Not yet decided, flagged, recommendations given but not confirmed

- **Duration cap.** Recommend capping how long a user can mark themselves free (for example a few
  hours maximum), so this stays a real-time, expiring signal rather than becoming a standing
  always-on broadcast, which would carry different and arguably worse risk (a stale but still-visible
  "here's where to find me"). Not confirmed with Elvis.
- **Account-standing threshold, exact number.** See room creation above, a concrete minimum needs
  Elvis's call, not proposed as final here.
- **Room auto-archival.** Recommend a user-created pinned room automatically closes or drops off the
  visible list after some period with no actively-free members (to avoid zombie rooms cluttering the
  map), while the one WePop-created citywide room per city never expires. Not confirmed.
- **Org accounts creating a room.** Marking yourself personally "free" is inherently an individual
  action, but whether an Organization account (a venue, a bar) could create a pinned room as a
  promotional draw ("come hang out, people are here right now") was not asked. A real, plausible use
  case, worth its own follow-up rather than assuming either way.
- **Spinning a Free Now conversation into a real Idea or Event.** Not designed. A lightweight
  connection (a room's plan turning into an actual scheduled event) is a natural future enhancement,
  parked, not blocking this scoping.

## Flags for Deepak, implementation, not decided here

- Moderation tooling (report, block, rate limiting) is not optional for this feature, treat as a
  required baseline given the documented abuse pattern in comparable open location-based chat
  features, not a nice-to-have to scope separately later.
- Location rounding needs an actual implementation choice (grid-snapping to a fixed-size area, a
  minimum display radius, or similar), a "rounded" requirement is not self-implementing and needs a
  concrete method chosen before build.
- The reciprocal join model (mark yourself free to see others) needs to be enforced server-side per
  room, not just hidden in the client, the same caution already flagged for the mutual-follow checks
  in item 8.
- DEC-002's existing age gate applies to Free Now the same as everywhere else in the app; no separate
  age-gating logic is being introduced here.
