01What Wepop is

Wepop is an invite-first, location-based events and meetup app: a tool for getting people together in the real world around shared activities. It is deliberately a meetup app, not a dating app, and much of the product design exists to hold that line (the anti-stalking visibility model, attendee-list gating, the no-paid-boost rule). Focus launch markets are Korea and the US.

It is being rebuilt on top of an existing Wepop codebase, salvaged and extended with AI-assisted build (DEC-008). That pushes technical choices toward reuse: recurring events are linked copies rather than a new multi-date object, so every existing per-event screen keeps working.

Invite-first

New users arrive through an invitation to a specific event or host, or wait on a waitlist that auto-promotes. Both a growth model and a real safety mitigation, cited as structural protection in the age-gate reasoning.

DEC-012, DEC-024

Real-world-first

The app pushes people toward attending, hosting and remembering real events (moments, ratings, check-in, cohorts) rather than browsing people. Discovery is about activities and who you already know.

02Core objects

Everything in the app hangs off five objects.

Event

A concrete activity at a place and time, with details, a Discussion board, media and chat. Standalone, one occurrence of a recurring group, or a member of one or more Series. Has a seven-status lifecycle (handoff spec section 3) including planning, live, completed and deleted, with a 60-day expiry for a planning event that never gets a date, and becomes largely immutable once completed to protect its rating record.

DEC-021, DEC-022, DEC-043

Idea

Something a user wants to do but is not hosting. Others rally around it (Interested, Discussion, time/place polls) and can spin a real Event out of it. No fixed date; behaves like a subreddit for a topic, with its own lifecycle. No media upload on Ideas.

DEC-009, DEC-040

Event Series

A host-created thematic hub page (cover, title, description, tags), not itself joinable but likeable, shareable and discussable, with events attached over time. Like an Idea but with a locked add-permission. Phase 1.5.

DEC-022

User profile

The person: birthdate, neighborhood, gender, languages, personality and interest tags, university, followers, created events/ideas, saved items and moments. Public in phase 1 (private accounts deferred).

DEC-005, DEC-015

Business / Organization profile

A multi-member account. University clubs first, promotional accounts later. Ownership transfers cleanly as officers turn over, and enforcement now propagates from a user to the orgs they operate.
